# Section 6: Idea Ranking & Evaluation

This document presents the **scoring model and ranking** for the **Top 8 candidate concepts** evaluated across eight strict dimensions (scored 1–5, max score **40**):

1. **Problem Importance (PI)**: Severity and dollar value of the problem.
2. **User Demand Evidence (UDE)**: Empirical proof from GitHub issues, HN, Reddit, and arXiv.
3. **Technical Feasibility in 36h (TF)**: Deliverable by a 3-person team without dedicated GPUs.
4. **Novelty (NOV)**: Differentiation from existing solutions and generic hackathon tropes.
5. **Demo Impact in <3 min (DI)**: Visual drama and "wow" factor for judges.
6. **Alignment with Hackathon Directions (AHD)**: Fit with PayPal's stated themes and sponsor technologies.
7. **Chance of Winning (CW)**: Probability of securing Grand Prizes or multiple Sponsor Prizes.
8. **Post-Hackathon Potential (PHP)**: Commercial and open-source viability beyond the competition.
